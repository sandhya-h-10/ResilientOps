Privacy

How information is used in this prototype.

Information you submit

Demo, contact and company-access requests save your company email, company name, optional message, request type, timestamp and signed-in user ID. Email delivery is not connected.

Sign-in and display

The hosting platform provides the signed-in identity. The application displays your name, or email when a name is unavailable.

Browser storage

The application stores simulation progress and your theme preference in this browser. No advertising or analytics tracking has been added by this application.

Policy contact

A formal privacy policy, retention schedule and privacy contact have not been supplied. A contact address will be added when available.
