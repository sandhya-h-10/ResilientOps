Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about how ResilientOps works, who it's for and what the demo includes.

Getting started

What does ResilientOps help me decide?

Which disruptions could affect production, which orders are exposed, and which recovery option to submit for approval.

How do I open the application?

Choose Sign in and continue through secure login. You'll go straight to the operations dashboard.

Is the demo using real company data?

No. The manufacturer, operational records and incident evidence are illustrative. The application clearly labels assumptions and any calculations that aren't available.

Planner workspace

What can a Planner do?

Investigate disruptions, review exposed orders, compare recovery options and prepare versioned plans for external approval.

Can I switch to another role?

No. This prototype provides only the Planner workspace. Approval requests can be tracked, but approval decisions must come from an external workflow.

Plans and approvals

Does approving a plan execute it?

No. Approval applies to a specific plan version. Execution must be requested separately, and its outcome is recorded separately.
