Help center

Follow an incident from investigation to an accountable decision.

Investigate a disruption

Register or detect an incident in Overview. Review its evidence and affected entities, then choose Register Disruption. On Impact Assessment, click Assess Impact. Download the impact report as PDF or open an email draft.

Prepare a recovery plan

Choose Evaluate Recovery Plans from the impact report. Compare timing, costs and constraints, select an option, then download the recovery planning report as PDF or text. Email opens a draft; attach the PDF manually.

Use the report for learning

After selecting a recovery option and generating its report, choose Start tracking. Tracking begins with execution marked Not started. Record execution status, action updates, shipments, material receipts, production progress, costs and customer delivery against that versioned plan.

Track outcomes

Capture completion or failure and lessons learned in Learning. Material deviations return to Impact Assessment. Choosing a plan does not approve or execute operational actions.

Review activity by calendar period

In Learning, choose Daily, Monthly or Yearly and select a calendar period. Review recorded activity across incidents, assessments, recovery plans and tracking. Dates use IST. Preview or download the activity report as PDF, or open an email draft and attach the PDF manually.

Change appearance

The Light/Dark button in the application top bar remembers your preference. Landing-page sound is optional and starts off.
