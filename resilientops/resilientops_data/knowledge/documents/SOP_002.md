# Interplant transfer planning

Document SOP_002 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Trigger

A dated component shortage has been calculated.

## 2. Procedure

Find compatible lots and permitted directional transfers. Simulate donor demand, protect stock, retrieve the route offer and add dispatch preparation, transit, clearance and release times exactly once. Compare receipt time with the shortage timeline. Calculate costs and request approval under POL_002.

## 3. Completion

Return source and destination plant IDs, lot/material IDs, quantity, timed arrival, cost currency, evidence, plan version and remaining donor balance. Execution remains outside the agent.
