# Emergency procurement policy

Document POL_001 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Scope

Applies to all three simulated plants when inbound supply threatens dated production requirements. A risk alert alone does not authorize a purchase.

## 2. Approved source requirement

Recovery candidates must use an APPROVED sourcing_agreement for the exact supplier site, material revision and destination plant, valid on the proposed order date. Supplier-wide approval is insufficient.

## 3. Commercial and operational checks

Respect minimum order quantity, order multiple, processing lead time, available supplier capacity, transport time and receiving time. Quotes and available capacity are estimates until separately confirmed.

## 4. Unapproved sources

Do not automatically qualify a new source or relax approval. Return NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW with the proposed qualification work. No unapproved supplier purchase is executable in V1.

## 5. Authorization

TRANSFER actions follow POL_002. Automated execution of EXPEDITE, PURCHASE and RESEQUENCE is disabled until explicit action-specific approval rules and execution handlers are implemented. A transfer authorization must not be reused for these actions.

## 6. Evidence

Preserve sourcing_id, agreement validity, calculation_id, snapshot_id, objective version and proposed plan version. Any known missing required input blocks an unconditional recommendation.
