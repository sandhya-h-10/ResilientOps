# Demand surge replanning

Document SOP_004 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Trigger

A customer request changes quantity or due date.

## 2. Procedure

Version the request, rebuild customer allocations, reschedule within line capacity and working calendars, and explode the applicable BOM. Recompute material requirements before asking Recovery for supply options.

## 3. Completion

Return changes to production and supply, incremental costs and late commitments. Updating only the customer row is not a complete simulation.
