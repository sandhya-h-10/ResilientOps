# Component qualification and quality policy

Document POL_004 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Revision compatibility

BOM lines name exact material revisions. Revision A of a component is not implicitly substitutable with another revision or another material ID.

## 2. Supplier qualification

An active supplier identity is not sufficient qualification. Check sourcing_agreements for the site, component revision and receiving plant.

## 3. Lot holds

Hold only identified lot quantities; do not reduce physical stock when applying a hold. The prototype baseline has no holds and immediate release. A quality-hold scenario changes usability explicitly.

## 4. Release authority

Release requires a recorded quality decision in the simulated action workflow. Agents cannot declare an inspection passed. Missing inspection evidence leads to escalation.

## 5. Audit limits

Synthetic supplier audit profiles summarize configured data. They do not certify real processes, legal compliance, machine capability, financial health or business continuity.
