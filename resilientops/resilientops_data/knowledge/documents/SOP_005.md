# Alternate sourcing assessment

Document SOP_005 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Trigger

Primary supply cannot meet a dated requirement.

## 2. Procedure

Retrieve exact supplier-site/material/plant approvals. Confirm validity, minimum order, pack size, shared capacity and processing lead time. Include transport and receipt readiness. Identify whether the alternative shares the same exposed site or logistics hub.

## 3. Completion

Return feasible candidates and rejection reasons. If no approved option exists, return INFEASIBLE or NEEDS_HUMAN_REVIEW. Do not invent another supplier.
