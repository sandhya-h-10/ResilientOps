# Quality hold handling

Document SOP_003 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Trigger

A scenario or authenticated quality record identifies a held lot.

## 2. Procedure

Validate lot and quantity. Apply the hold without reducing physical stock. Identify affected reservations and production orders. Recompute usable stock and flag commitments. Record any proposed replacement supply separately.

## 3. Completion

Report affected orders, hold quantity, release assumption and missing inspection evidence. No automatic release is permitted.
