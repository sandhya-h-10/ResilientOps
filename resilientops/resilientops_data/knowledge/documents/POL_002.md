# Transfer and spending authorization policy

Document POL_002 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Thresholds

In the synthetic reporting currency INR, a TRANSFER amount greater than or equal to 0 and strictly below 500000 requires PLANNER_MANAGER approval under AR_001. Amounts greater than or equal to 500000 require OPERATIONS_DIRECTOR approval under AR_002. Lower bounds are inclusive and upper bounds exclusive.

## 2. Comparable costs

Calculate all action costs using the dated synthetic exchange_rates table. INR to INR is identity. The synthetic rates are scenario assumptions, not market quotes. Do not compare JPY, CNY and INR amounts without conversion.

## 3. Plan binding

Approval binds to incident_id, plan_id, plan_version, action payload hash, approving identity and expiry. A material quantity, route, cost or timing change requires a new decision.

## 4. Execution

Only the application action service may execute an approved simulated transfer. Revalidate stock, donor demand, route capacity and current policy. Use a unique idempotency key so retries cannot duplicate actions.

## 5. Boundary cases

Exactly INR 500000 is assigned to OPERATIONS_DIRECTOR. Negative amounts, unknown currency conversions, missing approver role or expired approvals are invalid.
