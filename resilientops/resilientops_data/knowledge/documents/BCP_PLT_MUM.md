# Continuity procedure for Mumbai

Document BCP_PLT_MUM | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Plant scope

PLT_MUM represents a fictional electronics plant in Mumbai; timezone Asia/Kolkata, local currency INR. Calendars are weekday-only and omit national holidays.

## 2. Production dependencies

Configured capabilities: PRD_001 on PLT_MUM_L1, PRD_002 on PLT_MUM_L2, PRD_003 on PLT_MUM_L1. Read the released plant-specific BOM and dated schedule before calculating impact.

## 3. Recovery sequence

Validate the incident, calculate the no-action baseline, investigate approved alternates and interplant transfers, and compare timed recovery outcomes. Preserve donor commitments and component qualification.

## 4. Escalation and continuity

No feasible plan must be reported with exposed production/customer commitments. Do not promise customer delivery from a production completion alone. Approval thresholds follow POL_002; operational execution requires the action service.
