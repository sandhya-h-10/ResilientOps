# Inventory protection policy

Document POL_003 | Version 1.0 | Effective 2024-01-01

Fictional internal document for the ResilientOps simulation. This is not an actual supplier audit, legal policy or certification.

## 1. Usable stock

Usable stock equals physical quantity less mutually exclusive quality_hold_qty and other_blocked_qty. Reservations represent commitments and are not physical inventory movements.

## 2. Protected quantity

Inventory policy records currently protect 40 units per plant-material revision. These are synthetic minimums, not industry standards. Check the dated demand trajectory as well as the threshold.

## 3. Donor eligibility

Transfers must leave donor requirements serviceable throughout the planning horizon and satisfy minimum protected stock. Free stock at this instant does not prove transferable stock.

## 4. Demand accounting

Use usable stock and gross scheduled demand once for the material balance. Do not subtract reservations and then deduct their associated production demand a second time.

## 5. Quality conflicts

A new quality hold may invalidate an existing reservation. Flag affected production and recompute the baseline. Unknown quality status cannot be assumed released.
